## 1. Question and design

Does the reliability of the filings behind a fundamentals-based signal carry information about cross-sectional returns, directly (T1, T3) or as a moderator (T2)? Frozen rich point-in-time
panel (139,292 name-dates, 404 validation dates over eight yearly expanding-window folds), label `fwd_rank_21`, ridge alpha 10, boosted settings from EXP-010A. Four disclosure-process
variables built from the SEC filing registry; fourteen arms; thirty fits. Details and the decision rule are in the preregistration.

## 6. What this does and does not say

**What it says.** In this panel, adding six point-in-time disclosure-process columns to the 76 features of the ridge model raises mean Rank IC from 0.0149 to
0.0200 (+0.00515; HAC t 2.68; Holm-adjusted p 0.011). The same six columns permuted
across firms within each date add nothing (P0 averages 0.0146, the level of A1), so the gain is not a generic effect of having more columns. The registered rule returns SUPPORTED.

**What it does not say.**

1. *It is not stable across time.* It is absent in the two most recent validation years (folds 6 and 7: -0.0121 and -0.0007). Six of eight folds is the minimum the rule allows.
2. *Part of it is a persistent firm trait, not timing.* The shifted placebo keeps about half of the gain (P0s - A1 = +0.00273), and A5 beats it by only
   +0.00242 (t 1.28, p 0.10, six of eight folds). The rule asks only for a positive margin, which is met; it does not show that the time-varying
   part is significant. These variables move slowly, so a one-year shift preserves much of a firm's profile.
3. *It is a linear-model result.* The boosted learner gains nothing measurable from the block (S3 +0.00154, t 0.77, 4 of 8 folds), and the boosted arms are far
   stronger in absolute terms (Rank IC 0.033 and 0.034 against 0.020). Nothing here suggests a change to the strongest model.
4. *It does not translate into the frozen portfolio.* B1 net Sharpe at 10 bp is -0.49 for A5 against -0.34 for A1; the ridge portfolios are negative,
   the unfitted momentum rank N0 is +0.39 and the boosted arms about +0.54. Economics are exploratory and no inference is drawn from them.
5. *Moderation failed (T2).* The 48 interaction terms lowered the ordering (-0.00361, positive in 1 of 8 folds). The weak power of T2 (section 7) means this is weak evidence against moderation.
6. *The existing lag and staleness variables (T3)* point the way the timeliness literature does (+0.00268) but are not significant after adjustment (Holm 0.117).
7. *Exploratory status.* The same eight folds have been used by many earlier experiments (1,029 cumulative trials before this one); the adjustment covers only these three tests. A single supported test on reused folds is a lead, not a finding.
8. *The new block is not purely about filings.* It includes a count of missing fundamentals and a composite that contains two variables already in the rich set. Which of the six columns carries the gain is not
   known from this design; see section 8.

## 7. Registered sensitivity versus the standard errors the run produced (a post-hoc check, stated plainly)

The preregistration estimated the standard error of each paired difference from a label-shuffled rehearsal. With the real label the standard errors came out larger:

| Test | Standard error in the shuffled-label rehearsal | Standard error realised | Ratio | Smallest effect detectable with 80% power at the realised error (single test to strictest Holm step) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | 0.00102 | 0.00192 | 1.9x | 0.0048 to 0.0057 |
| T2 | 0.00231 | 0.00241 | 1.0x | 0.0060 to 0.0072 |
| T3 | 0.00021 | 0.00171 | 8.1x | 0.0043 to 0.0051 |

The registered detection thresholds were therefore too optimistic for T1 and T3. The T1 effect (+0.00515) is close to the threshold the realised noise implies, and an estimate that only just clears
a threshold tends to overstate the true effect. The registered decision rule and verdicts are not changed by this; the interpretation should be.

## 8. What this leaves open

- Which of the six columns carries the T1 gain (the composite `rel_u`, lag deterioration, the missing-fundamentals count, or the nearly constant amendment variables).
- Whether the effect returns, or was a feature of the earlier years, for the period the sealed holdout covers (not examined; not available to this study).
- Whether a richer measure of the disclosure process (late-filing notices, non-reliance events), which the filing registry used here does not contain, behaves differently.
