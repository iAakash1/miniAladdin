Economic caveats recorded with the map:

* `capital_expenditure`: a payment: reported positive
* `equity`: fallback includes non-controlling interest
* `net_income`: ProfitLoss includes non-controlling interest; fallback only
* `shares_outstanding`: dei cover-page shares are absent from the bulk data sets after ~2014; see security master

Rate definitions: *fallback rate* = share of (filing, period) facts whose chosen tag is not the primary tag (high for `revenue` because
`RevenueFromContractWithCustomerExcludingAssessedTax` only exists after ASC 606, and near 100% for `shares_outstanding` because the
`dei` cover-page count is not in the bulk `num` files after about 2014; see the security-master shares vintage for the API-sourced cover count).
*Tag-disagreement rate* = share of (filing, period) facts where two tags of the family carry different values; where the tags are different
economic quantities (`ProfitLoss` versus `NetIncomeLoss`, equity with versus without non-controlling interest) a disagreement is expected.

## 4. Rules that are load-bearing

1. **No random coalescing.** Priorities are fixed and versioned; a fallback is used only if a higher-priority tag is absent from that filing and period.
2. **Vintages are never overwritten.** A 10-K/A or later filing is a new row; `FIRST_REPORTED` and `AS_OF(decision_time)` are views.
3. **Availability is the acceptance time**, in US Eastern wall-clock time; accepted at or after 16:00 (or on a non-session day) means the next session.
4. **Missing is missing.** Nothing is filled, defaulted or zero-substituted.
5. `period_start` is derived from `qtrs` and labelled `derived_from_fsds_qtrs`; it never overwrites a source context.

## 5. Known limits

* The bulk data sets carry no XBRL `frame`, no `decimals`, and no per-context `dimensions` beyond the `segments` string; dimensional facts are excluded rather than modelled.
* Multi-class companies report shares by class as dimensional rows; they are excluded from the bulk-derived balance-sheet share count and handled (summed and flagged) only in the API-sourced cover-page vintage.
* `EntityCommonStockSharesOutstanding` is fetched per company from the SEC `companyconcept` API and dated by the FSDS acceptance time of its accession; an accession not present as a periodic filing in the registry is dropped.
* The SEC states the `sic` and `name` in `sub` are as of the filing date; that is a statement by the source, not something re-verified here beyond the change-frequency check in `docs/RICH_PIT_PANEL_AUDIT.md`.
